## How this site makes money

Every link from this table out to Amazon or eBay carries an affiliate tag. If
you buy something after following one, the marketplace pays us a commission —
usually a small percentage of the sale. **You pay exactly the same price either
way.** The commission comes out of the marketplace's margin, not out of your
pocket.

That is the entire business model. There are no ads, no sponsored placements,
no paid inclusion, and nothing on this site is for sale to a manufacturer or a
retailer.

## Required wording

As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.

We also participate in the eBay Partner Network, an affiliate programme that
allows us to earn fees by linking to eBay.

## What it does not change

**Ranking.** The table is sorted by price per terabyte, computed by one function
from the price and capacity the marketplace API reports. Commission rate is not
an input to it and no seller, brand or marketplace can pay for position.

**Inclusion.** Which listings appear is decided by the ingest job's category
filters, a seller-quality floor, and whether the specification could be resolved
confidently. Nobody pays to be in the table and nobody can pay to be left out of
it.

**The numbers.** Prices and shipping costs come from the official marketplace
APIs. We do not adjust them, round them in our favour, or hold a price on screen
after it has expired.

## What it does change, and you should know

We earn more when you buy a more expensive drive, and we earn nothing when you
decide not to buy anything. That is a real incentive, and the only honest
protection against it is that the site is useless the moment the numbers stop
being trustworthy. A wrong figure costs a visitor permanently; a commission is
worth a fraction of a percent of one sale. The maths is not close.

You should still check the price on the retailer's own page before buying. Ours
is a snapshot, refreshed at least every 24 hours, and prices move.

## Tracking

Following an outbound link from here sets cookies and identifiers belonging to
Amazon or eBay so they can attribute the referral. Those are the marketplace's,
governed by the marketplace's own privacy policy, and they operate on the
marketplace's site rather than on this one. See the
[privacy policy](/legal/privacy).

Every outbound link is marked `rel="nofollow sponsored noopener"`, which is the
disclosure search engines read.
