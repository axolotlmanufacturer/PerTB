## The short version

There are no accounts and no passwords on this site. We do not ask for your
name, we do not know who you are, and the only personal data we ever hold is an
email address you have deliberately given us for price alerts.

## What we collect

**Nothing, by default.** Browsing the comparison table sets no cookies of ours
and stores nothing about you.

**Analytics, only if you agree.** We use [Plausible](https://plausible.io), a
privacy-focused analytics service that is cookieless and does not build
cross-site profiles. It records page views, referrer, country, and a coarse
device type — aggregated, with no identifier that follows you. It does not load
at all until you accept it on the consent banner, and declining is remembered in
your browser rather than on our servers.

**Error reports.** We use Sentry to capture crashes so we can fix them. A report
includes the URL, the error and a stack trace. It is not used to track you and
carries no advertising identifier.

**Your email, if you ask for alerts.** Deal alerts are email-address-only. We
store the address, the filter you asked to watch, and the threshold. It is used
to send you that alert and nothing else — never sold, never rented, never added
to a marketing list. Every message carries a one-click unsubscribe, and
unsubscribing deletes the record.

## What happens when you click through

Following a link to Amazon or eBay hands you to them. They set their own
cookies and identifiers, including ones that attribute the referral to us, and
from that point their privacy policy governs, not ours:

- [Amazon Privacy Notice](https://www.amazon.com/privacy)
- [eBay User Privacy Notice](https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/member-behaviour-policies/user-privacy-notice-privacy-policy)

Those affiliate cookies are why this site has a consent banner at all. Our own
analytics is cookieless; theirs is not.

## Hosting and logs

The site runs on Vercel and the database on Neon, both in the United States.
Vercel keeps standard server logs — IP address, request path, user agent — for a
short retention period as part of operating the service. If you are in the EU or
the UK, your data is therefore transferred to the United States under the
relevant transfer mechanism.

## Your rights

If you have given us an email address you may ask us to show you what we hold,
correct it, or delete it, and we will. Depending on where you live you may also
have rights to object to processing or to receive your data in a portable form.
Unsubscribing from an alert deletes the record outright, which is the fastest
route.

## Children

This site is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from
anyone under 16.

## Changes

We will update the date at the top of this page when this policy changes.
