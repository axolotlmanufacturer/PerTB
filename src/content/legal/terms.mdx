## What this site is

A price comparison table. It reads listing data from the official Amazon and
eBay APIs, works out a price per terabyte for each one, and links you to the
marketplace to buy. We are not a retailer, we hold no stock, and we are not a
party to any transaction you make.

## Prices are a snapshot

Prices, shipping costs and availability come from marketplace APIs and are
refreshed on a schedule. A listing we can no longer refresh is removed rather
than shown stale — which is why the table can empty rather than show you an old
number.

Even so, **the price you see here may not be the price you pay.** Marketplace
prices move continuously, sellers change shipping terms, stock sells out, and
eBay listings are frequently single-unit. Always confirm on the retailer's own
page before buying. Where they disagree, they are right and we are out of date.

## Specifications

Capacity, recording technology, form factor and interface are derived by parsing
listing titles and descriptions against a curated table of drive families,
because the marketplaces do not supply reliable structured data for any of them.

We withhold a listing rather than publish a specification we cannot resolve
confidently, and an axis we cannot determine at all is shown as "—" rather than
guessed. Even so, parsing can be wrong. Confirm anything that matters — capacity,
CMR versus SMR, interface — against the manufacturer's own datasheet before you
buy.
